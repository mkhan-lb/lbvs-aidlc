## Scope and evidence

Based on `api`, `connectors` and `core`, with `data-layer` and `queuing` to explain shared contracts and processing. The alphabetical term-and-definition approach follows the [AWS AI-DLC v2.8.0 glossary](https://github.com/awslabs/aidlc-workflows/blob/v2.8.0/docs/guide/glossary.md); AWS remains the reference for lifecycle vocabulary, this file for organisation and product vocabulary.

**Source-backed** means the inspected source supports the definition. **Interpretation** means business wording or semantics still require a specific decision. Neither label means human-approved or verified in production. Every cited file was checked against its recorded commit; source links require access to the private repositories. Deployment state, tenant settings and current product policy have not been verified.

## Words to use carefully

- **Order:** API name: Order. External class: SalesOrder. Internal class: OrderType.
- **Shipment:** The external Shipment type maps to an internal ASN representation.
- **POC:** Product Onboarding Center in the product domain; proof of concept in a delivery discussion.
- **Workflow:** Commerce document processing is distinct from an AWS AI-DLC workflow.

## Definitions to reconcile with the product glossary

Dropship · Company / COID / Retailer / Supplier · POC / Product Management Center · Product feed · Document lifecycle · Document status / Workflow · Business rules / Maps / Document Standards · Backorder / Order lines / Warehouse inventory — the review decision for each is in the [comparison](logicbroker-glossary-comparison.md#definitions-to-reconcile). Business models (Marketplace, 1P, 3PL Fulfilled, B2B, D2C), product capabilities, operational vocabulary, EDI transaction sets (850, 855, 856, 810, 846, 832, 860, 812) and product-process terms (PIB, Epic, previews, GA) are missing here and are to be added from the product glossary after owner validation.

## Coverage limits

- COAT and COMB roles are evidenced, but their official expansions and current branding need confirmation.
- OrderNumber, PartnerPO, SupplierPO and source/destination keys need integration-owner review before adopting universal definitions.
- This draft does not enumerate every connector, supported EDI transaction set, tenant configuration or deployed workflow.
- The portal, account, workflows and other repositories were not independently audited. Their roles are included only where the inspected repositories describe them.
