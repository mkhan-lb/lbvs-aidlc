## Terms

- Acknowledgement (Order acknowledgement; ORDER_ACK; Orders & fulfilment): The supplier-facing order-processing concept represented by ORDER_ACK in Stock & Orders.
- Attribute (Product attribute; AttributeValue; Product induction): A named product characteristic defined within a data-model report.
- Backorder (BACKORDER; back-order quantity; Orders & fulfilment): An order or line state for items on back order.
- Buyer (Retailer; Organisations & relationships): The purchasing retailer in Virtualstock’s retailer–supplier relationship.
- Cancellation (CANCEL; CANCEL_ACK; Orders & fulfilment): The request to cancel an order or quantity, and its subsequent acknowledgement.
- Catalogue (Product catalogue; retailer catalogue; Product induction): The collection of product records associated with a retailer and its suppliers, organised into categories.
- Category (Product category; data-model category; Product induction): A classification used to organise products and their data requirements.
- Client (clients.Client; Organisations & relationships): The code name for a retailer organisation.
- Customer (customers.Customer; OMS customer; Organisations & relationships): In the Stock & Orders model, customers.Customer is a retailer-side record linked one-to-one to clients.Client through hub_client.
- Data model (datamodels.Model; DMEdit model; Product induction): A named, version-labelled definition of product-data structure.
- De-enrolment (Unpublish; UNPUBLISH; UNPUBLISHED; Product induction): The product workflow’s removal-from-publication concept.
- Debit note (DebitNote; Invoicing & returns): An invoice-linked financial document carrying its own number, date, parties, line-related totals and order references.
- Delta (ProductDelta; product change; Product induction): A product change record classified as new content, changed content, price, stock, image, discontinuation or a WACKO price change.
- Despatch (Dispatch; DISPATCH; shipment; Orders & fulfilment): The sending of ordered goods by the supplier, represented by the DISPATCH status and despatch/tracking details.
- Discrepancy (Discrepancies Approved; APPROVED; Orders & fulfilment): An order issue requiring resolution, such as a price discrepancy.
- Dropship (Drop shipping; dropshipping; Orders & fulfilment): A fulfilment arrangement in which a retailer places an order and a supplier sends the goods to the end customer.
- End customer (Shopper; delivery recipient; Organisations & relationships): The person or organisation receiving goods ordered through the retailer.
- Export process (ExportProcess; ProductExportProcess; Integrations & operations): A tracked operation that produces an export file, with product exports represented by ProductExportProcess.
- Free stock (free_stock; available stock; Stock & availability): The product availability value obtained through a configured report map or the free_stock product-data field.
- Hub supplier (suppliers.Supplier; hub_supplier; Organisations & relationships): The supplier record referenced by orders as hub_supplier and linked from the OMS supplier through a one-to-one relationship.
- Import configuration (Configuration; ImportMap; Product induction): The retailer/supplier/category-scoped definition of how incoming columns become product fields, including conversions and defaults.
- Import process (ImportProcess; ImportProductsProcess; Product induction): A tracked data-import operation, including product imports, used to record progress and manage user-triggered work.
- Invoice (invoice_service.Invoice; Invoicing & returns): The supplier’s billing document associated with a Virtualstock order, with billed customer, supplier, currency, amounts and external order references.
- Invoice status (draft; open; paid; uncollectible; withdrawn; deleted; Invoicing & returns): The invoice’s own state.
- Line item (OrderItem; order line; Orders & fulfilment): An individual item on an order, carrying SKU, quantity, price and its own status.
- OMS supplier (oms_suppliers.Supplier; Organisations & relationships): The order-management supplier record linked to the hub supplier and used by Order.supplier.
- Order (Purchase order; Order; Orders & fulfilment): The Stock & Orders record of a retailer’s order to a supplier.
- Order number (order_number; Orders & fulfilment): The retailer’s identifier for an order to a supplier, as described by the Order model.
- Order reference (order_ref; retailer internal reference; Orders & fulfilment): The retailer’s internal reference for an order.
- Order status (order_status; item_status; Orders & fulfilment): A state value recorded on an order or line, covering processing, fulfilment, cancellation and returns.
- Partnership (Retailer–supplier settings; suppliers.Settings; Organisations & relationships): The relationship between a retailer and a supplier for which platform settings and integration behaviour are configured.
- PIM (Product Information Management; Product Induct; Platform & terminology): The product-data capability represented by the-edge-product-induct: importing, structuring, validating, reviewing and publishing product information.
- Product (products.Product; Product induction): A retailer- and supplier-associated product record with a part number, category, flexible data, validation errors and publication state.
- Product induction (Product onboarding; Product induction): The process of bringing supplier product information into the retailer’s required structure, validating it and progressing it through review and publication.
- Product stage (ProductStage; staged product; Product induction): A publishing record containing a product snapshot, state and change flags, keyed by retailer, supplier and part number.
- Product status (status; sub_status; Product induction): PIM records both a high-level product status (Draft or Published) and a more detailed workflow sub-status such as Incomplete, Ready to publish or Publishing.
- Publication history (ProductPublishHistory; Product induction): The record of product publishing outcomes, including a publishing status, filename, errors and creation time.
- Published (PUBLISHED; Product published; Product induction): A product status or publishing outcome indicating publication within the relevant PIM workflow.
- Report map (ReportMap; field definition; Product induction): A configurable product-field definition containing its column, label, group, validation type, requiredness, visibility, editability and publishing conversion.
- Retailer (Client; buyer; Organisations & relationships): The business placing orders with suppliers and defining the product information and catalogue needed for its channels.
- Return (orders.returns.Return; RMA; Invoicing & returns): A return record for an order item and quantity, scoped to a retailer and supplier, with reason, method, status and collection details.
- RMA (Return merchandise authorisation; return authorisation; Invoicing & returns): The return-related acronym used in existing order fields and return code.
- SKU (Stock keeping unit; part_number; item_sku; Product induction): An item identifier.
- Split line (OrderItemSplit; Orders & fulfilment): A quantity-bearing portion of an OrderItem with its own status, references and backorder, cancellation or return details.
- Stock & Orders (SO; S&O; THE EDGE; Platform & terminology): The Virtualstock backend represented by the-edge, covering order flow, fulfilment, despatch, invoicing, returns and retailer/supplier integrations.
- Supplier (Vendor; fulfilment party; Organisations & relationships): The business that provides product data, reports stock and fulfils retailer orders.
- Tracking (OrderTracking; tracking number; proof of delivery; Orders & fulfilment): Delivery tracking associated with an order, including tracking number, carrier, URL, status, delivered flag and proof-of-delivery reference.
- Validation level (validity_level; validation_level; Product induction): Numeric levels stored on product records and configurable report fields to support product-data validation.
- Variant (Parent product; child product; Product induction): A product related to a parent or child product.
- WACKO prices (WACKO; delta_wacko; Product induction): An internal publishing-change category labelled “Wacko Prices”, with comparison fields for cost, RRP and markup prices.
- Workflow level (WorkflowLevel; UserWorkflowLevel; WorkflowLevelDecision; Product induction): A retailer-specific, ordered product-review level with a condition and designated users.

## Naming traps

- **Customer:** The Customer model represents the retailer side; “end customer” means the recipient.
- **Supplier:** suppliers.Supplier and oms_suppliers.Supplier are linked but distinct records.
- **Published:** Product state, transmission and retailer acceptance need separate definitions.
- **Stage / workflow:** PIM publishing and approvals use these words differently from AI-DLC.
