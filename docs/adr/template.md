# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD (original decision date when back-filling; say so)
- Owners: team or named decision owners
- Related: change ID, Jira key, PR, Confluence page, contract

## Context

The decision that must be made, the boundary it affects and the constraints. If this section passes ten lines, the decision is probably two decisions.

## Options considered

Realistic alternatives with their material trade-offs, including the one chosen. "Do nothing" counts when it was realistic.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly, with version or scope where relevant.

## Consequences

- Must: binding outcomes for future work.
- Must not: what this decision forbids.
- Follow-up: migration, verification and ownership work, each with an owner or a change ID.

## Evidence and rollback

What proved the option (measurements, spike findings, prototypes) and the safe path back if the decision turns out wrong.
