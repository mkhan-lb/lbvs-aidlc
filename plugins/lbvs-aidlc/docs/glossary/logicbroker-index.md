# Logicbroker glossary index

Generated by `scripts/build_plugin.py` from `logicbroker-glossary.md` in this directory; do not edit. One line per term: term (aliases; domain): first sentence of the definition. The full entry, its usage note and its evidence are in `logicbroker-glossary.md`.

## Terms

- Acknowledgement (PO acknowledgement; POAckType; Ack; Documents & fulfilment): A response document linked to an order, containing acknowledgement details and AckLines.
- API (Logicbroker API; Commerce API; Platform & terminology): The service in Logicbroker/api that hosts external commerce endpoints, internal portal endpoints, inbound AS2 and other integration endpoints.
- API script (ClearScript script; custom endpoint; Connectors & integration): A script configured through COAT and exposed as a custom API endpoint, commonly used for incoming webhooks.
- AS2 (Applicability Statement 2; Connectors & integration): An electronic business-document transport supported by the API for inbound connections and by the EDI queue for outbound transmission.
- ASN (Advance ship notice; AdvancedShipNoticeType; Documents & fulfilment): The internal shipment-notice representation mapped to the external Shipment API type.
- Backorder (AckType.Backorder; Documents & fulfilment): A response type in the acknowledgement contract indicating that fulfilment is backordered.
- Business rule (ClearScript rule; Connectors & integration): A script-based document-manipulation rule implemented by LogicTec.Core.BusinessRules and used in the workflow queue and API, with testing surfaces in COAT/COMB.
- Catalog attribute (CatalogAttribute; Catalog & inventory): A product-catalog field definition with an internal name, display label, type, grouping, options, visibility and validation rules.
- Catalog product (CatalogProduct; Catalog & inventory): A catalog entry identified by an ID and carrying product attributes, modification time and any error message.
- COAT (Internal configuration tool; Platform & terminology): The internal configuration surface referenced by connectors, workflow definitions, API scripts, business rules and document-export configuration.
- COMB (Operational tooling; connector debugger; Platform & terminology): The operational tool referenced for connector debugging, map inspection, document viewing, searchable files and queue-node management.
- Company (Account; CoId; company ID; Companies & identity): A business account used as context for platform documents and operations.
- Connector (BaseClient implementation; Connectors & integration): An adapter that transfers documents, inventory or product data between Logicbroker and an external API.
- Connector name (ConnectorName; Connectors & integration): The identifier declared on a connector and used in discovery, document tables and workflow configuration.
- Connector property (ConnectorProperty; configuration property; Connectors & integration): A connector setting with an internal key, display label and options controlling its configuration interface.
- Core (Logicbroker/core; Logicbroker.Core; Platform & terminology): The shared repository and package family containing foundational document types, external API contracts and mappings used by other Logicbroker services.
- Custom action (CustomAction; Connectors & integration): A connector method exposed as a button on its portal settings screen, used for actions such as generating diagnostic reports.
- Data layer (Logicbroker/data-layer; Platform & terminology): Shared libraries for transactional and configuration data access, inventory, validation, business rules, templating and document conversion.
- Destination key (Identifier.DestinationKey; Companies & identity): A destination-side reference carried in the API EntityIdentifier alongside source, Logicbroker and link keys.
- Document (Business document; internal document; Documents & fulfilment): A structured business record exchanged or processed by the platform, including orders, shipments, invoices, returns and acknowledgements.
- Document status (StatusCode; status ID; Documents & fulfilment): A document’s processing state, represented by StatusCode in the base API contract.
- Document type mapping (Internal/API type conversion; Documents & fulfilment): The explicit correspondence between internal document classes and API classes: OrderType→SalesOrder, AdvancedShipNoticeType→Shipment, InvoiceType→Invoice, RMAType→Return and POAckType→Acknowledgement.
- Dropship (IsDropShip; Documents & fulfilment): A fulfilment designation carried on an order line by the nullable IsDropShip field, identifying a drop-shipping arrangement.
- EDI (Electronic data interchange; Connectors & integration): Structured electronic business-document exchange supported by Logicbroker’s EDI parsing, mapping and transmission libraries and workers.
- Events queue (Activity events; Queues & processing): The worker area handling system activity events such as document-status changes, alerts and manual updates, including downstream triggers and automated emails.
- Extended attribute (ExtendedAttribute; KVP; DataAttribute; Documents & fulfilment): Additional named data carried beside standard document or line fields through arrays of DataAttribute values.
- Import / export (Get / Post document methods; Connectors & integration): The transfer of data into or out of Logicbroker.
- Inventory item (LBInventoryItem; Catalog & inventory): A supplier/merchant-associated item carrying SKU references, reported quantity, prices and warehouse availability information.
- Inventory queue (LogicTec.Core.Messaging.Inventory.V1; Queues & processing): The worker area for inbound inventory and catalog files and their persistence into storage and search indexes.
- Invoice (Invoice; InvoiceType; Documents & fulfilment): A billing document with invoice number, date, total and invoice lines, extending the shared order-related document fields.
- Item identifier (ItemIdentifier; Catalog & inventory): The set of identifiers used to recognise an item across systems, including supplier, partner, parent, manufacturer and external SKUs and other identifiers.
- Link key (Identifier.LinkKey; Companies & identity): A reference used to associate related business documents.
- Logicbroker key (LogicbrokerKey; document ID; Companies & identity): The platform identifier used to address an individual business document.
- Map (Mapping template; Handlebars map; Connectors & integration): A transformation between Logicbroker types and an external format.
- Merchant (IsMerchant; MerchantId; Companies & identity): The merchant-side trading party distinguished from the supplier in partner and inventory contracts.
- OAuth connection (IOAuthClient; connect / disconnect; Connectors & integration): The authorisation mechanism supported by connectors implementing IOAuthClient, exposed to users through portal connect and disconnect controls.
- Order (SalesOrder; OrderType; sales order; Documents & fulfilment): The order document exchanged through the API, carrying order lines, dates, shipping requirements and shared commercial references.
- Order line (OrderLine; line item; Documents & fulfilment): An item-level record with identifiers, quantity, unit of measure, line number, prices and fulfilment-related data.
- Order number (OrderNumber; Documents & fulfilment): An order reference carried by order-related API documents, alongside separate PartnerPO and SupplierPO fields.
- Partner (Trading partner; Partner; Companies & identity): A business counterpart represented by company name and ID, merchant/supplier role, vendor codes, partnership status, integration type and groups.
- Partner PO (PartnerPO; Documents & fulfilment): The purchase-order reference identified as PartnerPO on order-related documents, separate from OrderNumber and SupplierPO.
- POC (Product Onboarding Center; Catalog & inventory): The product-onboarding capability exposed by ProductOnboardingCenterController and associated catalog-manager APIs for product attributes, sets and categories.
- Portal (Customer portal; Platform & terminology): The user-facing application consuming internal API endpoints and providing connector setup and related document/configuration views.
- Product feed (Legacy product feeds; Catalog & inventory): The older product-posting arrangement described in the connectors README as using the inventory queue.
- Queue message (LogicTec.Core.Messaging.V1.Message; Queues & processing): The work envelope passed to a backend processor, with handler identification, body, properties and retry metadata.
- Return (Return; RMAType; Documents & fulfilment): A return document with return number, date, order references and return lines.
- Route queue (LogicTec.Core.Messaging.Route.V1; Queues & processing): The worker area that triggers workflows and other scheduled tasks.
- Sender / receiver (SenderCompanyId; ReceiverCompanyId; Companies & identity): The two company roles carried by the base document contract to identify its sender and receiver.
- Shipment (Shipment; ASN; AdvancedShipNoticeType; Documents & fulfilment): A document describing shipped goods, with shipment number, dates, shipment lines and freight references such as bill of lading and PRO number.
- SKU (SupplierSKU; PartnerSKU; MerchantSKU; Catalog & inventory): An item reference whose namespace depends on the party or system.
- Source key (Identifier.SourceKey; Companies & identity): The source-system reference carried in a document’s identifier set, separately from its platform and link keys.
- Supplier (SupplierId; vendor; Companies & identity): The supplier-side trading party, distinguished from the merchant by the partner contract and supplier identifiers on inventory items.
- Supplier PO (SupplierPO; Documents & fulfilment): A supplier-side purchase-order reference field on the shared order-related API entity, separate from PartnerPO and OrderNumber.
- Validation rule (Document validation; document standard; Connectors & integration): A rule checking business documents, supplied by Logicbroker.Validation for cases simpler than script-based business rules and shown on the portal’s document-standard page.
- Warehouse availability (WarehouseQuantity; NextAvailableQuantity; NextAvailableDate; Catalog & inventory): Inventory information for an identified warehouse, including quantities and future availability fields.
- Webhook (WebhookDetails; inbound webhook; Connectors & integration): An HTTP event-integration mechanism.
- Workflow (Document workflow; workflow runner; Queues & processing): A configured business-processing sequence executed by the workflow queue, including document import/export and connector operations.
- Workflow queue (LogicTec.Core.Messaging.Workflow.V1; Queues & processing): The worker area executing document workflows and workflow-runner jobs configured through COAT.

## Naming traps

- **Order:** API name: Order. External class: SalesOrder. Internal class: OrderType.
- **Shipment:** The external Shipment type maps to an internal ASN representation.
- **POC:** Product Onboarding Center in the product domain; proof of concept in a delivery discussion.
- **Workflow:** Commerce document processing is distinct from an AWS AI-DLC workflow.
